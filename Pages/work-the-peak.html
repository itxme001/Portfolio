The brief

A story-led vertical edit about an ascent, where the audience had to feel the climb in under a minute.

The approach

  • Mapped the story into three beats (doubt, effort, release) before touching the timeline.
  • Used pacing changes, not effects, to carry the emotion: longer holds in doubt, faster cuts in effort.
  • Finished with a clean mix so the final silence lands.
