Terms of Service

The terms that apply when you work with Xavier.

Scope of work

Each project is agreed in writing before work begins, including deliverables, timeline, and price. Anything outside that scope is quoted separately.

Payment

New clients pay a 50% deposit before work starts, with the balance due on delivery unless otherwise agreed. All prices are in USD.

Revisions

The number of revision rounds depends on the package selected. Additional revisions can be added at an agreed rate.

Ownership

On full payment, you own the final deliverables. I may show the finished work in my portfolio unless we agree otherwise in writing. Third-party assets (stock footage, music, fonts) remain under their own licenses.

Liability

I will do the work with professional care. To the extent permitted by law, my liability is limited to the amount paid for the affected project.
