Apex Digital: full brand rebuild

A complete digital transformation for a Dubai-based agency: website architecture, brand system, and motion package.

340%Lead increase
1.2sLoad time
98Lighthouse score
1Unified brand system

The challenge

Apex's website was slow, inconsistent with their brand, and hard to update. Leads were arriving, but the site was not helping convert them.

The approach

I rebuilt the brand foundations first (positioning, color, type), then designed and hand-coded the site on top of them. A custom CMS gave the team control over content without touching code, and an animation system added polish without hurting speed.

The result

Faster pages, a consistent identity, and landing pages built to convert. Lead volume rose sharply after launch while load time dropped to around a second.
