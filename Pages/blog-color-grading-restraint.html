My Color Grading Philosophy: Why Less Saturation Creates More Impact

Cinematic color is mostly about restraint. Here is how I decide what to leave out.

Color scienceFeb 2026

Start with a clean base

Before any look, make the footage consistent: correct exposure, balance white, and match shots. A style applied to inconsistent footage only makes the inconsistency louder.

Protect skin tones

Skin is the anchor of most images. If skin looks natural, viewers will forgive bold choices elsewhere. I keep skin in a narrow, believable range and push the look into shadows, highlights, and the background.

Lower saturation, raise contrast of ideas

Over-saturated footage fights for attention. Pulling saturation back lets one color, one light, or one subject stand out. Impact comes from contrast between what is muted and what is not.

Check it on a phone

Most viewers will see your grade on a small bright screen. Review on a phone, in different lighting, and make sure the image still reads when it is tiny and the brightness is high.
